# PulseKey privacy policy

PulseKey is created by [@BraxFFA](https://linktr.ee/BraxFFA).

PulseKey does not collect personal information, send telemetry, create an account, or contact a server automatically. Its settings are stored on your device at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PulseKey\settings.txt`. Macros are saved as `.pulse.xml` files only when you choose to save them, at the location you choose.

PulseKey reads the titles of visible desktop windows to populate its target picker. It uses that information locally to direct and pause input; it does not send window titles elsewhere. Clicking the @BraxFFA credit opens the linked website in your browser, where that site's own privacy practices apply.

To remove PulseKey data, close the app, delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PulseKey\settings.txt`, and delete any `.pulse.xml` files you saved. Uninstall the app through Windows if you installed it from the Microsoft Store, or delete the portable files if you downloaded it from GitHub.

For questions about this policy, contact [@BraxFFA](https://linktr.ee/BraxFFA).
